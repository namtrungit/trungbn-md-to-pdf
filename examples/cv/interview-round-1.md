# Vòng 1 — Background check và culture fit

Buổi phỏng vấn chủ yếu bằng tiếng Anh. Vòng này hỏi lý lịch và cách làm việc với người khác. Chưa hỏi code sâu.

Trước buổi phỏng vấn, đọc lại resume một lượt. Chuẩn bị ngày bắt đầu làm được và mức lương muốn nói. Hai số này không có trong resume.

## Câu nên học thuộc

### 1. Tell me about yourself.

I am Trung. I am a software engineer in Ho Chi Minh City. I have worked for more than eight years. I am strongest in React and TypeScript. I also build APIs with Go and Ruby on Rails. Now I work on a hospital purchasing system. Before that, I built a portal for hospitals and suppliers. I like clear screens, clean code, and I check my work before release.

### 2. Walk me through your resume.

From 2018 to the end of 2018, I was an intern. I built screens with Ext JS and APIs with Ruby on Rails. From 2019 to 2025, I worked on MIMS. Hospitals and suppliers use one website to manage implant items, prices, and stock. I built the React screens, the database, and the Rails APIs. Since 2025, I have worked on EDI Authority. Hospitals send purchase orders. Suppliers send replies. I built the React screen and the Go API, so buyers can see price and quantity differences.

### 3. Why are you looking for a new job?

I have learned a lot in my current job. Now I want a new team and a new product. I want to use English more at work. I also want to grow from writing features to helping with design and reviews.

### 4. Why do you want this job?

The role matches my experience. I have built React screens and backend APIs for real users. I also like the interview process, because the second round uses a work sample. I can show how I work, not only talk about it.

### 5. What are you good at?

I am good at React and TypeScript. I break a big screen into small parts, and I test them. I also read the requirement carefully. If something is not clear, I ask early.

### 6. What is a weak point?

I sometimes spend too long making the code clean. Now I set a time limit. I finish the useful version first, then I clean the important parts only.

### 7. Tell me about your current project.

I work on EDI Authority, a system that helps hospitals manage buying and paying for medical supplies. Think of it as a "checker" between the hospital and its suppliers. When a supplier sends an order or an invoice, the system checks if the prices, quantities, and items match the contract. If everything is correct, it goes through automatically. If something looks wrong, it sends it to a person to review. This helps hospitals avoid paying too much and saves a lot of manual checking.

Bản ngắn, nếu họ hỏi tiếp phần kỹ thuật:

I work on EDI Authority. A hospital sends an order. A supplier sends a reply. My screen shows the differences in price, quantity, unit, and item number. The buyer can see the problem and decide what to do. The frontend is React. The API is Go. Kafka moves the files. OpenSearch is used for search.

### 8. How do you work with other people?

I work with business analysts, testers, and other developers. First we agree on the requirement. Then I estimate the work. When I finish, I show the result and I listen to feedback. I also gave product demos to customers.

### 9. Tell me about a problem at work.

After one release, search results looked wrong. I checked the sync job and the API. I compared the database and the search data. Then I fixed the bad data and checked the result again. I also watched the job after the next release.

### 10. Tell me about a mistake.

Early in a task, I started coding before the rule was clear. Later we had to change the screen. Now I write a short note first: what we will change, and which files we will touch. I ask someone to confirm it. Then I code.

### 11. How do you use AI?

I use Cursor and Claude Code. The AI writes the first draft of the design, the code, and the review. I read it and I decide. If it is wrong, I do not use it. A person must approve each step. I do not copy the answer without checking.

### 12. How is your English?

I can talk about my work in simple English. I sometimes need a slow speaker, or I ask people to repeat. I can read documents and write short updates. I want to use English every day so I can improve.

### 13. When can you start?

I need time to hand over my current work. I think I need about one month. I can confirm the date after I speak with my manager.

Đổi "one month" nếu công ty hiện tại yêu cầu thời gian khác.

### 14. What salary do you expect?

I am open to a fair offer for this role and my experience. May I know the salary range for this position?

Nói số chỉ khi họ hỏi lại lần hai. Chuẩn bị một số trước buổi phỏng vấn.

### 15. Do you have questions for us?

Yes. What does the team expect in the first three months? In the work sample, what do you want to see: clean code, a working feature, or both? Will the second interview be about my sample?

## Cách nói khi bí từ

- I am sorry. Can you say that more slowly?
- Let me think for a few seconds.
- I understand the question. Here is a short answer.
- I do not know that word. Can you explain it?

## Vòng này chưa cần kể

Không cần giải thích Kafka, Redis, hay từng mã lỗi 850 và 855, trừ khi họ hỏi. Nếu họ hỏi kỹ thuật sâu, nói: "I can explain the idea. We can go deeper in the technical round."
